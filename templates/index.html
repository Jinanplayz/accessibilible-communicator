Assistive Speech System

Type it. Hear it.
In your own voice.

VoiceFlow clones your voice from a short recording, then turns anything you type into speech that sounds like you. There's no account to create. Record once, then start typing.

When you want someone else to hear you, open a live room and share the link. They keep that tab open in the background, with no chat window to read and nothing to check. When you type and press Speak, your voice plays on their device, the same way it would if you'd said it yourself.

Who this is for

Someone with vocal cord dysfunction (VCD) can lose their voice mid-sentence during a flare-up. Instead of the conversation stalling or switching to text messages, they can type the rest and keep talking, out loud, in their own voice. Someone with Tourette's whose tics make sustained speech difficult or unpredictable can type at their own pace and have it spoken clearly and continuously, without interruption. Either way, the person they're talking to doesn't need to watch a screen. They just listen, like always.

Record at least one minute

A full minute of clear, natural speech gives VoiceFlow enough to work with to build an accurate clone. Read anything: a recipe, a news article, your own thoughts. The content doesn't matter, just keep talking.

If speaking continuously for a minute isn't possible right now, that's okay. You can also upload an existing recording (a voicemail, a video, anything with at least a minute of your voice in it).
